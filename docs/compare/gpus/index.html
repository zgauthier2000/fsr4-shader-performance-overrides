How far these numbers go

Measured: report counts per GPU name, and download counts per archive file (GitHub's own counters).

Estimated: machines. Downloads are not people either: one person may fetch both the zip and the .7z, or every release.

Not known: which GPU a download was for inside a family, and anything from 20,570 downloads of the older archives (one desktop and one integrated archive, single DLLs, test builds), which mix families. Timing kit downloads (2,590) are left out of the family chart.

RX 6000 is about half of the kit machines but about a third of the per-family downloads. A likely reason (a guess, not measured): most open kit questions were about RX 6000 speed, so those owners were asked to test most. The kit shows who helps test; the downloads come closer to who uses the builds.
