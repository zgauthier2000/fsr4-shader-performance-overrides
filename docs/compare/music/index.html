Cyboman Computer Music 2

Fourteen songs about making FSR 4 upscaling faster: frame skip, timing runs, overnight trainings, full drives and one very fast GPU with free time.

Press play on any song. Drag the bar to jump around. The lyrics follow along as it plays.

Words written by cyboman with Claude (an AI assistant). Music, arrangement and vocals of every song except "Thank You" were generated with ACE-Step 1.5 (MIT license), run locally on a Radeon RX 7800 XT. "Thank You" is spoken with Qwen3-TTS (Apache 2.0) over wind, rain and thunder made in code.

The lyric timing was found by speech recognition, so a line is missing here and there. All songs were made the same loudness (EBU R128). Free to share.

From the FSR 4 shader performance overrides project.
