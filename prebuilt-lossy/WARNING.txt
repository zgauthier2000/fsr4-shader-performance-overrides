WARNING: THIS VERSION CHANGES THE PICTURE.

It is NOT the same as the "exact" version of this project, and NOT the same as AMD's file.

- The exact version produces exactly AMD's picture, byte for byte. This one does not.
  If you want AMD's picture, use the exact version.

What it does
- It runs FSR 4's neural network only on every other frame. On the frames in between it
  reuses the network's last result, moved along with the picture's motion, and relies almost
  entirely on the previous picture. Where the picture is not moving, those frames take
  nothing from the new frame, so fine detail at rest shimmers no more than with AMD's file.
- Three kinds of pixel take the new frame on those frames all the same: ones a moving object
  has just uncovered, ones that scrolled in from outside the screen, and ones whose content
  changed without the game reporting motion (particles, sparks, embers).
- The network's arithmetic itself is AMD's, unchanged. (Earlier lossy versions also
  simplified it; this one does not.)

What it costs
- A still picture is about 0.2 dB less accurate than AMD's; thin lines lose a little more.
- Strips that a moving object has just uncovered are less sharp for one frame, most of all
  when the camera pans fast against the object's motion.
- On skipped frames the result depends on the game's motion vectors. Where they are wrong,
  it can be taken from the wrong place.
- Small sparks are shown slightly dimmer on skipped frames than with AMD's file.
- Frame times alternate: a skipped frame is about 1.7 ms shorter than a normal one on an
  RX 7800 XT at 4K. With a frame cap or normal V-Sync the average is what counts; with
  low-latency modes or unbuffered V-Sync the longer frames can miss.
  https://github.com/zgauthier2000/fsr4-shader-performance-overrides/blob/main/docs/frame-pacing.md

Good to know
- Frame skip turns itself off in Ultra Performance (render size under half the output size)
  and on frames the game marks as a reset. It picks frames from the game's camera jitter; a
  game with an unusual jitter pattern may skip unevenly.
- It is opt-in and experimental.
- These are the Linux files: use them with AMD's ORIGINAL DLL and the launch option
  VKD3D_SHADER_OVERRIDE='Z:/path/to/fsr4-shader-performance-overrides/prebuilt-lossy' %command%
  OptiScaler then shows plain 4.1.1 (AMD's DLL is in use).
- Built from vkd3d-proton's translation on an RX 7800 XT (GE-Proton 11-7). Like the exact
  prebuilt files they do not fit every setup; if the picture looks wrong, do not use them.
